Privacy Notice

Your data never leaves your iPhone.

Effective date: September 29, 2026 · Last updated: September 29, 2026

Summary in one paragraph

PCS Mobile's WealthPath is a fully on-device personal finance app for iOS. It does not require an account, does not connect to the internet for its core features, does not contain advertising, does not include analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not transmit your financial data to any server. Everything you enter — accounts, transactions, investments, budgets, goals, and receipt photos — is stored locally inside the app sandbox on your iPhone.

1. Information WealthPath does not collect

WealthPath does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of the following:

  • Account numbers, balances, or holdings
  • Transactions, categories, tags, or notes
  • Investment cost basis, prices, or unrealised gains
  • Budgets, goals, or progress data
  • Receipt photographs or any attached media
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or precise location
  • Usage analytics, crash reports involving third parties, or behavioural data

2. Where your data lives

All app data is stored locally on your iPhone inside the WealthPath app sandbox. If you have enabled iCloud Backup for your device, standard device backups may include app data according to your system-wide iCloud settings — this is controlled by Apple and your device configuration, not by WealthPath.

Receipt photos are written to the app's Documents directory. They are not exported to your Photos library unless you explicitly choose to save them from within the app.

3. Permissions requested by the app

PermissionPurpose
CameraCapture a receipt photo to attach to a transaction.
Photo Library (read)Pick an existing photo to attach to a transaction.
Photo Library (write)Save a captured receipt back to your Photos library, only when you request it.

WealthPath requests these permissions only when you tap the relevant action. You can decline any of them and continue to use every other feature.

4. No third-party SDKs

WealthPath contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, no attribution SDKs, no social-login SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no remote-configuration services. There is no JavaScript or WebView content that could contact a third party.

5. No bank linking, no aggregation

WealthPath does not connect to banks, brokerages, exchanges, or any financial institution. It does not use screen-scraping services, Open Banking APIs, Plaid, Yodlee, Finicity, or any equivalent service. There are no servers operated by PCS Mobile that receive your financial data.

6. Children's privacy

WealthPath is a utility app intended for users aged 16 and older. It does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

7. International users

Because WealthPath does not transmit personal data off the device, the cross-border data transfer obligations of regimes such as the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA, the LGPD, and the PIPL do not apply to WealthPath's operation. If you choose to back up your iPhone to iCloud or iTunes, the resulting backups are governed by Apple's privacy policies, not this one.

8. Data deletion

You can delete all WealthPath data at any time by deleting the app from your iPhone. Doing so removes the app sandbox and every file inside it, including receipts, accounts, transactions, investments, budgets, and goals.

9. Changes to this notice

If we ever change WealthPath in a manner that affects privacy — for example, by introducing an optional cloud sync feature — we will update this notice, raise the version number in the App Store, and prompt you to review the changes before any new data collection begins.
