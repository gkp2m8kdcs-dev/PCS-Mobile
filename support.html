Frequently asked questions

Does WealthPath require an account?

No. WealthPath does not require sign-up, sign-in, email verification, or any other form of registration. Install, open, and start tracking.

Does WealthPath connect to my bank?

No. WealthPath is a manual-tracking app. You add accounts and transactions yourself; nothing has been pulled from a financial institution.

Is my data private?

Yes. All data is stored locally inside the app sandbox on your iPhone. Read the full Privacy Notice for details.

How do I back up my data?

WealthPath relies on your device's standard iCloud or iTunes backup. There is no WealthPath cloud account. To move data to a new iPhone, restore from an encrypted device backup on the new device.

How do I delete all my data?

Long-press the WealthPath icon on your Home Screen, tap Remove App, then confirm. This deletes the app sandbox and every file inside it, including receipts.

Can I export my data?

Use the More tab → Export to save a JSON snapshot of your accounts, transactions, investments, budgets, and goals. This is useful before major iOS upgrades or device changes.

Investment prices look stale. Why?

WealthPath does not query live market feeds. Prices are entered manually and used to compute unrealised gains and asset allocation. Open the holding and tap Update Price whenever you want a fresh value.

The camera permission prompt isn't appearing.

On iOS, prompts appear only when you tap the in-app camera button. If you previously denied access, open Settings → WealthPath and re-enable Camera, then return to the app.

Which currencies are supported offline?

Eighteen currencies including USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CNY, HKD, SGD, AUD, CAD, CHF, INR, KRW, MXN, BRL, ZAR, SEK, NOK, and NZD. Conversion rates are entered manually inside the app.

I lost data after an iOS update.

Please email Sitikhodijah00232@gmail.com with the approximate date and your device model. While we can't recover deleted app data, we can help you diagnose the cause and prevent it next time.

Troubleshooting checklist

  • Force-quit WealthPath and reopen it.
  • Make sure your iPhone is running iOS 16.0 or later (Settings → General → Software Update).
  • Free up at least 200 MB of device storage for receipt photos.
  • Disable any iOS content-filter profiles that might block the app's local storage.
  • Restart your iPhone.
