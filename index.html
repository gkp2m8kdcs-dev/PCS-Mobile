Everything a private finance app should be

WealthPath is built for serious investors who care about their data. Fast, customisable, and unafraid of detail — without giving up your privacy to a server.

All accounts in one place

Checking, savings, credit, brokerage, retirement, and crypto — grouped, tagged, and always at hand.

Investor-grade portfolio

Stocks, bonds, ETFs, mutual funds, crypto, real estate, and commodities with manual prices and live gain calc.

Goals & budgets

Savings targets, debt payoff, retirement, and emergency funds — with per-category budget rings and overspend alerts.

Receipts on-device

Capture or import receipts with the camera, stored locally inside the app sandbox and attached to any transaction.

18 offline currencies

Built-in currency conversion for eighteen currencies — works fully offline, no exchange-rate API required.

Crafted for iOS

A native, dark-themed iOS utility designed for App Store delivery on iOS 16 and later.

Charts that respect your touch

Every chart in WealthPath is hand-drawn in Core Graphics — no third-party trackers, no rendering engines phoning home.

Net Worth — interactive

Scrub through twelve months of net worth history with a finger.

Cash Flow — categorised

Monthly income vs. expense, side by side, per category.

Your money is your business.

WealthPath stores every account, transaction, receipt, and goal locally on your iPhone. No servers. No accounts. No analytics. No ad SDKs. No push notifications. No bank linking.
